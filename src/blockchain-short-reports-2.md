<h2>Blockchain short reports</h2>

<p>We have been publishing short reports since August 2019. The structure of these reports and the red flags raised can be considered as a “rough outline” of the methodology employed by the members of our community to determine whether the price of stock is likely to experience a considerable decrease in value in the short run. In order to profitably short sell the tickers featured in our reports, precise timing is a must. For this reason all of our short calls (short reports and trade alerts) have been time stamped with blockchain technology since February 2020. It serves as proof of the precision of our short calls.</p>

<p>The research and trading strategy of the tickers featured in our short reports is extensively discussed in our premium chat service hours/days in advance of the publication of the reports.
