		<p>Utopia Capital Research is a membership community of activist short sellers and veteran traders. We specialise in countertrend short trading small/medium cap USA publicly listed companies. Our approach is rooted in extensive technical analysis and due diligence that focuses on price volatility and the so-called "underhanded" practices of management and related third-parties. Precise timing and a deep understanding of fundamentals are crucial.</p>
<p>In August 2019, we started publishing short calls in the form of short reports, highlighting the fundamental red flags which we consider the most relevant. Since February 2020, we began publishing trade alerts in addition to our short reports, as well as time stamping all of our single ticker publications in order to prove the high quality of the timing of our short calls.</p><p><strong>We are always on the look out for the following red flags:</strong></p><ul><li>Artificially inflated stock prices.</li><li>False and misleading positive statements.</li><li>Accounting irregularities.</li><li>Bad actors in management or related affiliates.</li><li>Undisclosed related-party transactions.</li><li>Illegal/unethical business or financial reporting practices.</li><li>Undisclosed regulatory, product, or financial issues.</li><li>Financially distressed/shell risk.</li><li>Share issuance / dilution of shareholder value.</li><li>Foreign listed companies.</li></ul><p><strong>If you wish to join our community, please see our <u><a href="https://utopiacap.wordpress.com/premium-service/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">members</a>hip information and optio</u>ns.</strong></p>
