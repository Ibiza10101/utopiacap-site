						<div class="fusion-text"><h4 style="text-align: center;"><strong>Welcome to our Investor Warnings.&nbsp; Our Investor Warnings can be split into two different categories. The first one focuses on so-called “dubious” third parties such as funds, lenders and other entities that are involved with several publicly traded companies that raise one or several red flags. This content requires extensive research and provides key insight into the nature and performance of the “investments” of these third parties, consequently we have not made this information available to the public for free.<span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span style="color: #339966; text-decoration: underline;"> <a style="color: #339966; text-decoration: underline;" href="/2020/03/17/lincoln-park-capital/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Here is an example of what can be expected of these third party focused reports</a>. </span></span></strong></h4>
<h4 style="text-align: center;"><strong>The second category of Investor Warnings can be described as less detailed “<span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span style="color: #339966; text-decoration: underline;"><a style="color: #339966; text-decoration: underline;" href="/blockchain-short-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Blockchain Short Reports</a></span></span>“. They contain information about tickers which we believe to be good candidates for short selling and are also timestamped with blockchain technology. The blockchain timestamps are proof of the time of publishing and content of these articles as explained below.<br>
</strong></h4>
<h4><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>What are blockchain timestamps?</strong></span></h4>
<h4><strong>Blockchain timestamping creates a unique and universal fingerprint (called the hash) for every piece of content. If the input changes even the slightest, the hash becomes totally different. </strong><br>
<strong>This hash is added to the blockchain with a date and time. Because you have the input that results in this specific hash, you can prove that you had the content at that point in time.</strong><br>
<strong>If you are not familiar with Blockchain you can <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/blockchain.asp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #339966;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">read details here</span></span></a>.</strong></h4>
