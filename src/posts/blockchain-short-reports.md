						<h4 style="text-align: left;"><strong>Welcome to our Short Reports. Since late January 2020, all of our short reports are timestamped with blockchain technology.</strong><strong>You can now verify to the second when a new Short Report is published and the share price at that specific point in time. </strong><strong>The blockchain timestamps are proof of the time of publishing and content of these articles as explained below.</strong></h4>
<h4 style="text-align: left;"><strong>The companies featured on our short reports have been selected through a set of variables established by our proprietary machine learning AI and database,”Asgard”.&nbsp; Asgard could thus be described as stock a selection tool that identifies high-probability fraudulent/dubious tickers and ascertains high-profitability trading entry and exit points. For more information check out our <a href="/about/"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span style="color: #339966; text-decoration: underline;">about</span></span></a> page.</strong></h4>
<h4><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>What are blockchain timestamps?</strong></span></h4>

<h4><strong>Our content is timestamped with WordProof Timestamp, which is why you might see a notification that looks like this on some of our pages:</strong></h4>
<div class="imageframe-align-center"><span class="fusion-imageframe imageframe-none imageframe-1 hover-type-none"><img width="895" height="57" alt="" title="Wordproof blockchain timestamp" src="/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Wordproof-blockchain-timestamp.png" class="img-responsive wp-image-3720"><noscript><img src="/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Wordproof-blockchain-timestamp.png" width="895" height="57" alt="" title="Wordproof blockchain timestamp" class="img-responsive wp-image-3720"/></noscript></span></div>
<h4><strong>Because of this, you (as a visitor) can verify that what we wrote and published is actually shown on the webpage: it is impossible for us (or anyone else) to tamper with the information. For each page, a fingerprint is generated (the hash), which is added to a blockchain transaction. Because of the immutability of the blockchain, this transaction (and the fingerprint) can’t be modified or removed. Thus, you know for sure (and can verify yourself) that our content was written at the indicated time.</strong></h4>
