<h2>Blockchain trade alerts</h2>

<p>Since April 2020, we have regularly published trade alerts. Due to the volatile and fast paced nature of the stocks we short, it is oftentimes not possible to have a full short report ready in time for publication before the price of stock may drop significantly. Consequently, trade alerts allow us to warn our subscribers and members about short selling opportunities in a timely manner.</p>
<p>Our trade alerts are time stamped using Blockchain technology in order to prove the precision of their timing. Same applies to our short reports.</p>

<p><a class="btn-readmore" href="/track-record/">More about our track record</a></p>

<p><strong>In order to receive our trade alerts (and all other research and publications) immediately after they are published, please consider becoming a member. This will give enough time to carry out your due diligence and plan your strategy.</strong></p>
<p>The research and trading strategy of the tickers featured in our trade alerts is extensively discussed in our premium chat service hours/days in advance of the publication of the alerts. This gives our members ample time to analyze our research, carry out their due diligence and discuss potential trading strategies with other community members.</p>
