# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is very important to us. At Utopia Capital Research we have a few fundamental principles that we follow:

- We don't ask you for personal information unless we truly need it.
- We don't share your personal information with anyone except to comply with the law, develop our products, or protect our rights.
- We don't store personal information on our servers unless required for the ongoing operation of our site.

## What personal data we collect and why we collect it

### Comments

When visitors leave comments on the site we collect the data shown in the comments form, and also the visitor's IP address and browser user agent string to help spam detection.

An anonymized string created from your email address (a hash) may be provided to the Gravatar service to check if you're using it ([Gravatar's privacy policy](https://automattic.com/privacy/)). After approval of your comment, your profile picture is visible to the public alongside it.

### Embedded content from other websites

Articles on this site may include embedded content (videos, images, articles, etc.). Embedded content from other websites behaves exactly as if you had visited those websites directly, and may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with it.

## How long we retain your data

If you leave a comment, the comment and its metadata are retained indefinitely, so follow-up comments can be recognised and approved automatically instead of sitting in a moderation queue.

## What rights you have over your data

If you have an account on this site, or have left comments, you can request an exported file of the personal data we hold about you, and request that we erase it — except data we're obliged to keep for administrative, legal, or security purposes.

## Cookies

A cookie is a string of information that a website stores on a visitor's computer, which the browser provides back to the website on each return visit. We use cookies to identify and track visitors, their usage of the site, and their access preferences. If you don't want cookies, set your browser to refuse them — some features may not work properly without them.

## Analytics

Google Analytics is used to record website visitor statistics, used in aggregate to learn what content is useful to visitors. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Changes to this policy

Utopia Capital Research may change this Privacy Policy from time to time. Your continued use of this site after any change constitutes acceptance of that change.
